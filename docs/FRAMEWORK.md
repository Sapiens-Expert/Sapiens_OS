# Ten domains: inquiry map

Sapiens OS is a practical teaching scaffold, not a validated composite score. Every domain is invited in onboarding and the quarterly review. The person's priorities, safety, circumstances, and limiting constraint determine what to act on first.

| Domain | Ask about | Avoid assuming |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sleep | Opportunity, timing, quality, daytime impact, barriers | A tracker or a single night's duration establishes a disorder |
| Nutrition | Regular access to satisfying food, variety, timing, restrictions, feasibility | Weight or food choice reveals health or character |
| Movement | Enjoyable activity, everyday movement, strength, capacity, pain or access | More exercise is always safer |
| Stress | Demands, sources, coping, perceived load, changes in functioning | Every difficulty is a personal regulation failure |
| Social connection | Belonging, practical support, boundaries, isolation by choice or circumstance | Solitude always means loneliness |
| Light/dark rhythms | Daylight, schedule, evening environment, shift work | A rigid light prescription fits everyone |
| Agency | Choice, control over workload and routine, obstacles, ability to say no | Illness or structural limits reflect weak agency |
| Meaning | Values, roles, contribution, tensions and changes | A stated purpose resolves symptoms |
| Rest | Breaks, leisure, recovery, time away, demand reduction | Sleep alone compensates for chronic overload |
| Curiosity | Learning, play, openness, safe novelty | Curiosity is a treatment or obligation |

These domains interact. For example, excessive demands can displace sleep and social time; acting only on sleep habits may miss the work design. Ask what support or environmental change is possible. `EVIDENCE.md` distinguishes stronger public-health guidance from the framework's own unvalidated organization.
