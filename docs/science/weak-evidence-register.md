# Weak-evidence register

Popular claims that should not be repeated as settled. Use this before quoting a "science says" statement. An entry is not proof that the idea is false; it says what the sources actually found.

## "Willpower is a battery that drains" (ego depletion)

**Evidence tag: Contested.**

**What the sources found.** A preregistered multi-lab replication (23 labs, N = 2,141) found an effect of d about 0.04, with confidence intervals including zero [R29]. A multi-site preregistered test (36 labs, N = 3,531) found d about 0.06 and no evidence for a depletion effect, with Bayesian analysis favoring the null about four to one [R30]. Another multi-lab replication (12 labs, N = 1,775) found a small significant effect (d about 0.10, about 0.16 after exclusions) [R31].

**How the AI uses it.** Do not tell a person they have "run out of willpower". Fatigue, sleep, hunger and circumstances are still real; the specific resource-depletion mechanism is the weak claim. See [Energy and recovery](energy-and-recovery.md) for what is supported.

**When not to use this entry.** It does not say self-control is easy, or that tiredness has no effect on behavior.

## "Nudges reliably change behavior"

**Evidence tag: Contested.**

**What the sources found.** A 2021 meta-analysis reported d about 0.45 with moderate publication bias [R26]. A 2022 reanalysis found no evidence for an overall effect after bias correction [R27], and another reply reported much smaller bias-adjusted averages and wide variability [R28]. Some categories, such as structural changes to choices, appeared stronger than others in the original analysis [R26].

**How the AI uses it.** Treat environment design as a hypothesis to test in one person's life, not a guaranteed technique. See [Environment and commitment](environment-and-commitment.md).

## "Match teaching to your learning style"

**Evidence tag: Contested, leaning against.**

**What the sources found.** A synthesis of 17 meta-analyses reported d about 0.04 for studies that actually tested matching instruction to style, and noted that correlational studies often confuse styles with strategies [R32]. A 2024 meta-analysis found an overall benefit (g about 0.31) but only about a quarter of outcome measures showed the crossover pattern the hypothesis predicts, and judged the benefit too small and too infrequent to justify widespread adoption, citing low study quality [R33].

**How the AI uses it.** Ask what *strategies* help the person learn this task (retrieval, spacing, examples) and let them choose, rather than assigning a style label.

## "Premortems prevent failure"

**Evidence tag: Thin.** See [Decisions and forecasting](decisions.md). The popular claim runs ahead of the tested evidence, and one 2022 paper found a bias toward uncontrollable risks [R02].

## "It takes 21 days to build a habit"

**Evidence tag: Contested.** The framework's own [EVIDENCE.md](../EVIDENCE.md) already records that real-world automaticity varied widely and no single day count is justified for an individual. Do not state a number.
