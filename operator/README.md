# Operator pack

Sapiens OS framework developed by **Vladislav (Vlad) Andreev**. © 2026 Vladislav Andreev. MIT-licensed original code and text; brand assets excepted.

The core ten-domain framework helps a person notice and reflect. The operator pack adds a way to *run*: to decide, plan, protect attention, manage load and recover, on a rhythm the person controls. "Operator" means anyone who runs their own life and work: a founder, a manager, a freelancer, a parent, a student, a caregiver. Nothing here is specific to a job title.

It is optional and layered on the core. It keeps the same rules: consent for retention, `unknown` for what was not said, one or two experiments at a time, a stop condition on everything, and no clinical claims. See [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md).

## Principles

1. **Capacity before ambition.** A plan the person cannot sustain for months is not a plan. Start from current capacity, not from the target.
2. **Reduce load before adding method.** If demands exceed capacity, the first move is to drop, delay, delegate or renegotiate, not to add a routine.
3. **Evidence-tagged tools.** Every method comes from the [behavioral science library](../docs/science/README.md) with its strength stated. Weak or contested claims are named as such.
4. **The person decides.** The AI offers options and a recommendation, states the trade-off once, and respects the call.
5. **Rest and enjoyment are ends in themselves.** Not every hour has to be optimized. Never turn biology into a productivity obligation.

## The operating rhythm

| Cadence | What | Template | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Daily | A short start and a short close | [DAILY-START-SHUTDOWN](../templates/DAILY-START-SHUTDOWN.md) | about 5 minutes each |
| Weekly | The existing check-in, plus open decisions | [WEEKLY-CHECK-IN](../templates/WEEKLY-CHECK-IN.md) | 10 to 15 minutes |
| Monthly | Load against capacity, and what to stop | [MONTHLY-CAPACITY-REVIEW](../templates/MONTHLY-CAPACITY-REVIEW.md) | 20 to 30 minutes |
| Quarterly | Full ten-domain review | [QUARTERLY-REVIEW](../templates/QUARTERLY-REVIEW.md) | as needed |
| Annual | Direction, values, what to keep and end | [ANNUAL-REVIEW](../templates/ANNUAL-REVIEW.md) | one sitting, no rush |
| On demand | A consequential decision | [DECISION-RECORD](../templates/DECISION-RECORD.md) | 10 minutes |

None of these runs by itself. A reminder or schedule is created only with consent, through a service the person controls, as described in [LIFECYCLE.md](../docs/LIFECYCLE.md). Skipping a cadence is not a failure; the AI should ask whether it still fits.

## Operating context (asked once, revisited quarterly)

Add these to onboarding only if the person wants the operator layer. Every question is optional.

- What do you run or look after, and who depends on you?
- Which decisions do you carry that other people cannot make for you?
- When is your attention most fragmented, and what would you protect if you could?
- What can you not change right now (hours, money, contracts, care duties)?
- What would you stop doing if you could?
- What does enough look like this season?

Record answers in the private operating note, not in this repository.

## Boundaries

- Sustained overload with exhaustion or loss of function is a [SAFETY.md](../docs/SAFETY.md) matter, not a prompt to optimize harder.
- This pack does not provide financial, legal, tax or investment advice, and does not replace professional advice on any of them.
- It makes no promise about income, performance or health outcomes.
