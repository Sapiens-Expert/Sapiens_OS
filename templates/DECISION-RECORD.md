# Decision record (optional)

Private. Use for decisions you call important and cannot easily undo. Keep completed copies outside this public repository. The purpose is learning, not judging yourself. Evidence for decision records is thin ([decisions](../docs/science/decisions.md)); treat this as an experiment.

- **Date and decision:**
- **What I am choosing between:** (include "do nothing" and "wait")
- **Reversibility:** easy, costly or hard to undo? If easy, run a trial rather than a long analysis.
- **Expectation:** what I think will happen, and how confident I am (a rough number is fine).
- **Reference class:** similar past efforts of mine or others, and what actually happened. Where does my plan sit against them?
- **Reasons:** why this option, in a few lines.
- **Failure story:** it failed. Why? Which reasons could I influence? ([premortem is thin evidence](../docs/science/decisions.md); use it as a prompt, not a safeguard.)
- **What would change my mind:** the signal, and the date I will look for it.
- **Who is affected, and did I ask?**

## Review (at the date above)

- What happened?
- What did I expect, and how far off was I?
- What did I know then that I ignored, and what did I not know?
- What would I do differently next time? Keep a decision good if the process was sound even when the outcome was not.
