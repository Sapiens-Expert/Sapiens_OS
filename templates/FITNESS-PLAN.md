# Fitness plan and review (optional)

Private. Use with the [fitness path](../docs/paths/FITNESS.md). Keep completed copies outside this public repository. Skip any line. A missed week is not a lapse, only a week with no entry.

## Starting point

- **Check done?** I read the starting-point questions and nothing there means I should start with a professional first (yes / no / not sure). If not sure, I will ask a professional before I begin.
- **My goal, in my words:** (get fitter or stronger, build muscle, lose fat, both, keep what I have)
- **Why it matters, and what enough looks like:** (without a number if I prefer)
- **If both:** which leads for the next four weeks, and what the other gives up:
- **What I can run for six months:** (sleep, work, caring duties, money, access)

## One lever for the next four weeks

- **Lever:** resistance training, aerobic activity, food, or sleep and recovery. The others stay unchanged.
- **The small change, as a cue and an action:** "When / after X, I will Y."

- **Stop signs I agreed in advance:** (preoccupation with food, rigid rules, training through pain, sleep shortening, mood dropping, any new physical symptom)

## Four-week review

- What happened? (use the dashboard CSV if I recorded sessions)
- What did the change cost me, in sleep, time, mood and relationships?
- Do I want to keep, adapt, pause or stop?
- Is the goal still mine?
- What would a maintenance version look like? ([why plan this early](../docs/science/fitness-and-body-composition.md))

If a stop sign appeared or I want to pause, that is a valid result. See [SAFETY.md](../docs/SAFETY.md).
