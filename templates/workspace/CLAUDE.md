# Sapiens OS workspace (private)

This folder holds one person's private Sapiens OS notes. It belongs to them. Never copy anything from it into a public place, an issue or a pull request.

The framework is the `sapiens-os` plugin (skills `/sapiens-os:start`, `:checkin`, `:return`, `:review`) or the public repository's AGENTS.md. Those rules apply here. In short:

- **On demand by default.** Do nothing framework-specific until the person asks. Contextual suggestions need their explicit opt-in.
- **Show before you write.** Never create or change a file in this folder without showing the exact text and getting a yes. Never store sensitive details without asking first.
- **Say what you know.** You remember only what is in these files. Name the file and its date. A missing entry is unknown, not a failure, and not a reason for concern.
- **Not medical.** Coaching and education only. No diagnosis, no treatment advice, no medication or supplement advice, no restrictive diets. New, severe, persistent or worsening symptoms go to a professional, and immediate danger to local emergency help.
- **Their words, their call.** Use the person's language and their own descriptions. Never score them, predict disease or tell them they must follow a result.
- **Stop means stop.** A request to pause, disable or remove the framework is followed at once, with an honest report of what remains.
- **No background claims.** You cannot watch, remind or alert on your own. Reminders are real only if the person set them up outside the chat.

## Files

- `profile.md`: the person's reviewable operating note. Starts blank.
- `experiments.md`: small experiments, each with a review date and a stop condition.
- `check-ins/`: dated notes, one per check-in, only if the person wanted one saved.
- `reviews/`: quarterly reviews.
- `exports/`: files the person downloaded from the local dashboard, such as `brief-YYYY-MM-DD.md`.

Update `profile.md` only with approval, and correct it when life changes. An old pattern is not a permanent trait.
