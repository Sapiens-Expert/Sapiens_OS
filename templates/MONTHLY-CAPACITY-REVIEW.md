# Monthly capacity review (optional)

Private. A thirty-minute look at load against capacity. The aim is to lower load where it is too high, not to add method. Keep completed copies outside this public repository.

## 1. Load

List what you are carrying: projects, commitments, people who depend on you, recurring obligations. For each: is it **growing, steady or shrinking**, and did you **choose** it?

## 2. Capacity

In your own words: how were sleep, energy, recovery time and time with people this month? What changed because of workload? Mark each `better`, `same`, `worse` or `unknown`, only if you choose to. No score.

## 3. The gap

Where did demands exceed capacity, and what gave (sleep, relationships, health, quality, money)? Is the gap temporary (a known peak) or structural (it will continue)?

## 4. Stop, delay, delegate, renegotiate

Pick at most **two**. For each, name the cost of the change and the cost of not changing.

| Item | Stop / delay / delegate / renegotiate | Cost of changing | Cost of not changing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| | | | |

If everything seems necessary, say so, and ask what a person you trust would cut.

## 5. Decisions and forecasts

Which decisions from this month would you make differently? Which estimates were off, by how much, and what does that suggest for the next plan? (See [decisions](../docs/science/decisions.md).)

## 6. One experiment

At most one adjustment for next month, with a cue or boundary, a stop condition and a review date. It may be "no change".

## 7. Care threshold

If you described persistent exhaustion, reduced function, or something that worried you, check [SAFETY.md](../docs/SAFETY.md) before planning.
