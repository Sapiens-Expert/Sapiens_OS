# Daily start and close (optional)

Private. Keep completed copies outside this public repository. Skip any line. A missed day is not a lapse, only a day with no entry.

## Start (about 5 minutes)

- **Capacity today:** how much room do I realistically have (low, normal, high), and why?
- **What matters most:** one to three outcomes, in my words. Is anything here someone else's priority I have not chosen?
- **Hard parts:** is anything big enough to try a [reference-class check](../docs/science/decisions.md) (similar past efforts, how long they took)?
- **Protect:** one block of attention I will protect, and what I will let wait.
- **Floor:** if the day goes wrong, what is the smallest version that still counts?

## Close (about 5 minutes)

- **Where I stopped:** one line per open task, with the next action. This helps the next switch ([attention](../docs/science/attention-and-load.md)).
- **Tomorrow, specifically:** a short list of specific next actions. Optional: a trial of writing it close to bedtime ([evidence is thin](../docs/science/energy-and-recovery.md)). Stop if it raises worry.
- **Switch off:** my transition out of work (a walk, a note, closing the laptop). Did it work?
- **One thing that went right, and one thing to change.**

## Weekly review hook

Once a week, bring open decisions and anything that kept recurring to [WEEKLY-CHECK-IN.md](WEEKLY-CHECK-IN.md). If the close takes more than ten minutes or feels like a duty, shorten it or drop it. The routine serves the person.
