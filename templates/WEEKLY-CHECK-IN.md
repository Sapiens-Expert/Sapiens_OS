# Optional five-minute check-in

Date and period: __. Changes since baseline: __. What went well: __.

Ask: How are energy, sleep, mood, function, demands, recovery, food access, movement, and connection? Which other Sapiens OS domains matter this week? What changed outside your control? Any new, persistent, worsening, or severe concern? You may skip any question.

AI: compare only with available prior self-reports; cite dates. Ask before interpreting. Name any uncertainty and use `docs/SAFETY.md` for care thresholds. End with one feasible step, a stop condition, and a review date. Do not assign a risk score or claim to predict disease.

If the person opted into a routine experiment, ask: Did the cue happen? Did you do, adapt, skip or have no opportunity? Did the routine help, cost energy, or create pressure? Would you keep, simplify, change the cue, pause or stop? Were reminders welcome and timely? A missing log entry is unknown, not a failure.
