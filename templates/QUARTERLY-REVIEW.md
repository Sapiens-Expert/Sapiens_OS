# Quarterly extended review

Set a reminder in a calendar or automation you control; a repository cannot initiate contact. Do this review approximately every three months, or earlier if desired. Date: __. Prior baseline and review dates: __. Available information and gaps: __.

## Revisit the person and context

What changed in goals, work, finances, caregiving, environment, disability/access needs, treatment, or support? Which prior experiments helped, had no effect, or caused harm? What was the opportunity cost? Which goals now conflict with well-being?

## Cover all ten, one section at a time

For each domain, ask what has changed, its effect on daily functioning, what supports it, and what feels worth changing. Mark `better`, `about the same`, `worse`, or `unknown` only if the person chooses that description; these are reflections, not clinical scores.

## Synthesis with consent

Ask about interactions, especially high demands with insufficient recovery or a meaningful loss of function. Identify the likely limiting constraint as a hypothesis, and offer alternatives. Check `docs/SAFETY.md` before planning. Agree on one or two feasible changes, a person or resource to involve if needed, a measure meaningful to the owner, a stop condition, and a follow-up date. Show the complete draft; ask what to correct, delete, or retain. Never publish this completed review.
