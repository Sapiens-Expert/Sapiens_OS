# Annual review (optional)

Private. Do this in one unhurried sitting, or across a few. The question is direction, not performance. Keep completed copies outside this public repository.

1. **What happened.** The year in your own words: events, changes, losses, things you are glad of. Use the four quarterly reviews if you have them.
2. **What mattered.** What was worth it? What would you not trade? What cost more than you expected?
3. **Values and roles.** What do you value now, in words you would actually use? Which roles (work, family, community, self) got too much or too little?
4. **Keep, change, end.** Choose a few items in each column. Ending something is allowed and often the best result.
5. **A direction, not a target.** One sentence about where you want to be heading. If you add a goal, check it with the [goal-setting module](../docs/science/motivation-and-goals.md): is it yours, specific, and sustainable?
6. **Capacity floor.** What must stay protected, whatever else changes (sleep, a relationship, health care, a day off)?
7. **A fresh start, if you want one.** Pick a landmark and one small first step ([evidence is thin](../docs/science/environment-and-commitment.md)).
8. **Update the private note.** Correct old assumptions, delete what no longer fits, and approve the changes yourself.

If the year contained a loss, illness or crisis, take it at your own pace and involve human support if you want it. See [SAFETY.md](../docs/SAFETY.md).
